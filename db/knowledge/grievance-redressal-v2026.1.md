# Grievance Redressal Procedure
Synthetic document for the LoanLens AI hackathon demo. Not real bank policy.
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Applies to all Kestrel Bank retail loans

## Level 1 — Branch or Call Centre
- Call 1800-000-5353 (fictional number) or visit any branch.
- Complaints are acknowledged within 2 working days and resolved within 7 working days.

## Level 2 — Nodal Officer
- If the complaint is not resolved after 7 working days, email nodal.officer@kestrelbank.example.
- The Nodal Officer resolves the complaint within 15 working days.

## Level 3 — Principal Nodal Officer
- Escalate to principal.nodal@kestrelbank.example.
- The bank gives its final response within 30 days of the original complaint.

## Regulator
If the complaint is not resolved within 30 days, or the customer is not satisfied with the bank's response, the customer may approach the RBI Integrated Ombudsman.
