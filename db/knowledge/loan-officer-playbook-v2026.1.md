# Loan Officer Playbook
Synthetic document for the LoanLens AI hackathon demo. Not real bank policy.
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · INTERNAL — for Kestrel Bank staff only. Never share with customers.

## Processing Fee Waivers
Officers may waive up to 50% of the processing fee for salaried customers with a credit score of 780 or above.
Waivers above 50% need branch manager approval.
Never promise a waiver before the credit check is complete.

## Handling Borderline Applications
- An obligation ratio of 45% to 55%, or a credit score of 640 to 680, is borderline.
- Route the application to a credit analyst with a note.
- Suggest a longer tenure or a co-applicant to the customer.

## Requesting Missing Documents
Ask the customer once, in a single message that lists every missing document.

## What Not to Disclose
Never share internal scorecards, waiver limits or analyst notes with customers.
