# Demo Vehicle Loan Document Checklist

Synthetic testing policy for the LoanLens hackathon demonstration. This document is fictional and does not represent a real bank policy. It does not change product rates, eligibility rules or approval authority.

## Scope

This checklist covers new and used vehicle loan applications for all customer segments in the demo. Document submission does not guarantee approval. A human credit analyst makes the final credit decision.

## Identity proof

Provide one valid identity document, such as a PAN card or passport. For testing, use synthetic documents only. Do not upload real identity numbers or personal financial records.

## Address proof

Provide one current address document, such as a utility bill or rental agreement. An application may be saved while address proof is missing, but the missing document must be reviewed before a final approval decision.

## Income proof

Salaried applicants should provide a recent salary slip and bank statement. Self-employed applicants should provide an income tax return and bank statement. Student applicants should provide the income evidence requested for their co-applicant.

## Vehicle evidence

For a new vehicle, provide the dealer quotation. For a used vehicle, provide the seller quotation and a copy of the vehicle registration certificate. The officer should review the available evidence and request missing documents.

## Missing or unclear documents

The loan officer should request a readable replacement for an incomplete or unclear document. The customer should see a plain-language description of the missing item. Internal credit scores, risk signals and staff audit notes must not appear in customer messages.

## Final decision

Completing this checklist does not automatically approve a loan. The human credit analyst reviews the application, document checks and applicable product rules before recording the final decision.
