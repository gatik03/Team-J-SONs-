# General Loan Terms and Conditions
Synthetic document for the LoanLens AI hackathon demo. Not real bank policy.
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Applies to all Kestrel Bank retail loans

## Disbursal Timelines
Personal loans and vehicle loans are disbursed within 2–3 working days after approval and signing of the loan agreement.
Home loans are disbursed within 7–10 working days after approval, once the legal and technical verification of the property is complete.
Education loans are paid directly to the institution within 5–7 working days after approval, as per the fee schedule.

## Application Status Stages
- Draft: the application has been started but not yet submitted by the customer.
- Submitted: the customer has submitted the application and it is waiting for verification.
- Under Verification: the bank is checking the customer's KYC and uploaded documents.
- Under Review: a credit analyst is assessing the application against the credit policy.
- Approved or Rejected: the credit analyst has recorded the final decision and the customer is informed.
- Disbursed: the approved loan amount has been paid out.

## Key Fact Statement
Every borrower receives a Key Fact Statement with the interest rate, all fees and the total cost of the loan before signing the loan agreement.

## Final Decision
Every credit decision is made by a Kestrel Bank credit analyst. Automated tools only prepare recommendations.
