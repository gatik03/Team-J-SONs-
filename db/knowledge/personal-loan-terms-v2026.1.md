# Personal Loan Terms and Conditions
Synthetic document for the LoanLens AI hackathon demo. Not real bank policy.
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Applies to Kestrel Personal Loan – Salaried (PL-101) and Kestrel Personal Loan – Self-Employed (PL-102)

## Eligibility
- PL-101 (salaried): minimum net monthly income ₹25,000, minimum credit score 700.
- PL-102 (self-employed): minimum monthly income ₹40,000, minimum credit score 720, business vintage 3 years.
- Total EMIs including this loan must not exceed 50% of net monthly income.

## Interest Rate and Tenure
- PL-101: indicative rate 12.0% per annum (range 11.5% to 18.0%), tenure 12 to 60 months, loan amount ₹50,000 to ₹25,00,000.
- PL-102: indicative rate 14.0% per annum (range 13.0% to 20.0%), tenure 12 to 48 months, loan amount ₹1,00,000 to ₹15,00,000.

## Processing Fee
The processing fee is 1.5% of the loan amount, subject to a minimum of ₹2,500 and a maximum of ₹10,000, plus GST at 18%.
The fee is deducted from the disbursed amount and is non-refundable.

## Foreclosure and Part-Prepayment
- Foreclosure is allowed after 12 EMIs have been paid.
- Foreclosure charge: 3% of the outstanding principal.
- Part-prepayment is not allowed.

## Late Payment
- Penal charge of 2% per month on the overdue EMI amount.
