# Prepayment and Foreclosure Policy
Synthetic document for the LoanLens AI hackathon demo. Not real bank policy.
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Applies to all Kestrel Bank retail loans

## Home Loans
- Floating-rate home loans to individuals: no foreclosure or part-prepayment charges.
- Fixed-rate home loans: 2% of the outstanding principal if closed within 3 years; nil after 3 years.

## Education Loans
- No foreclosure or part-prepayment charges at any time.

## Personal Loans
- Foreclosure is allowed after 12 EMIs, with a charge of 3% of the outstanding principal (same as the Personal Loan Terms and Conditions).

## Vehicle Loans
Foreclosure of vehicle loans is governed by the Vehicle Loan Terms and Conditions in force.

## How to Request Foreclosure
- Request a foreclosure statement in the app or at any branch.
- The foreclosure statement is valid for 7 days.
- The closure letter and NOC are issued within 7 working days of full payment.
