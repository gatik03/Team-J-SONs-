# KYC and Document Checklist
Synthetic document for the LoanLens AI hackathon demo. Not real bank policy.
Version 2026.1 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Applies to all Kestrel Bank retail loans

## Identity Proof
- PAN card (mandatory).
- Plus one of: Aadhaar, passport, voter ID or driving licence.

## Address Proof
- One of: Aadhaar, passport, utility bill not older than 3 months, or registered rent agreement.

## Income Proof — Salaried Applicants
- Last 3 months' salary slips.
- Last 6 months' bank statement showing salary credits.
- Form 16 for the latest year.

## Home Loan — Self-Employed Applicants
Self-employed applicants for a home loan must submit all of the following documents.
- Income Tax Returns for the last 2 years with computation of income.
- Audited profit and loss account and balance sheet for the last 2 years.
- GST returns for the last 12 months (where GST registration applies).
- Proof of business existence for at least 3 years (GST registration, Shop and Establishment certificate or trade licence).
- Business and personal bank statements for the last 12 months.
- KYC of the applicant and of the business entity (identity proof, address proof, business PAN).
- Property documents (sale agreement, title deeds, approved building plan, and NOC from the builder or society).

## Home Loan — Salaried Applicants
- The salaried income proof listed above (salary slips, bank statement, Form 16).
- Property documents (sale agreement, title deeds, approved building plan, and NOC from the builder or society).

## Education Loan — Students
- Admission letter from the institution.
- Fee schedule of the course.
- Last mark sheets.
- Co-applicant KYC and income proof.
