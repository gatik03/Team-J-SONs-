# Credit Policy and Eligibility Criteria
Synthetic document for the LoanLens AI hackathon demo. Not real bank policy.
Version 2026.2 · Effective 2026-04-01 · Applies to all Kestrel Bank retail loans

## Credit Score Requirements
Credit scores range from 300 to 900.
- Home loan: minimum credit score 700 for salaried applicants and 720 for self-employed applicants.
- Personal loan: minimum 700 for salaried and 720 for self-employed applicants.
- Vehicle loan: minimum 650.
- Education loan: minimum 650 for the co-applicant.

## Income-to-Obligation Ratio
Total monthly EMIs, including the new loan, must not exceed 50% of net monthly income.

## Borderline and High-Value Applications
An obligation ratio between 45% and 55%, or a credit score between 640 and 680, is borderline.
Applications of ₹10,00,000 or more are high-value.
Borderline, high-value and policy-exception applications are always reviewed by a credit analyst.

## Policy Exceptions
A loan amount outside the product's minimum or maximum, a tenure outside the product range, or a customer segment not eligible for the product is a policy exception. Only a credit analyst can approve an exception, with a written reason.

## Factors Never Used in Decisions
Gender, religion, caste and marital status are never used in any credit decision.
